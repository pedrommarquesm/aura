# Auraclimb Final Brand System

Three logo variations for strategic use: Hybrid (primary), 3D Depth, and Gradient Glow.

---

## The System

You have three production-ready logos that work together as a system:

1. **Hybrid (3D + Glow)** - Primary app icon
2. **Variant 3 (3D Depth)** - Premium contexts
3. **Variant 8 (Gradient Glow)** - High-energy contexts

All maintain the moai (🗿) cultural connection while serving different strategic purposes.
