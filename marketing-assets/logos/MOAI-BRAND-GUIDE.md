# Auraclimb Logo - Moai Brand System

Your chosen brand identity: **Moai + Arrow** (Version 1) for everything.

---

## Why This Logo Is Perfect

### 1. Cultural Authenticity
The moai (🗿) is the universal symbol for aura in Gen Z culture. Your target audience sees this and immediately understands: "This app is for us."

### 2. Instant Recognition
No explanation needed. The moai emoji has billions of uses across TikTok, Instagram, Twitter. It IS aura culture.

### 3. Meme-Friendly = Viral
- Users will naturally use 🗿 when talking about your app
- "Just ranked up on 🗿"
- "Downloaded the moai app"
- Built-in shareability

### 4. Unique Position
Most social apps have abstract, minimal logos. You have a literal cultural icon. Unforgettable.

### 5. Perfect Scaling
The moai shape is iconic and recognizable from 16px to billboard size.

## Brand Don'ts

❌ **Never:**
- Call it "the moai app" in official communications (let users do that)
- Explain what moai means in marketing (if they know aura, they know moai)
- Use the emoji incorrectly (it's 🗿 not 🤖 not 🪨)
- Make the icon smaller than 40px on web (minimum for recognition)
- Stretch or distort the icon
- Add effects (glows, shadows, bevels)
- Change colors outside brand palette

✅ **Always:**
- Let the icon speak for itself
- Trust your audience knows the culture
- Use the moai emoji liberally in social content
- Maintain high contrast for visibility
- Export at correct sizes for each platform
