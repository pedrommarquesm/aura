# Moai Logo Variants Comparison

10 different stylistic approaches to the moai + arrow concept. All maintain cultural authenticity while exploring different design directions.

---

## Overview

Each variant keeps the core moai (🗿) concept but explores different:
- Compositional approaches
- Stylistic treatments
- Visual emphasis
- Artistic techniques

All variants work at app icon sizes (1024px down to 16px).

---

## The Variants

### 1. Side Profile View
**File:** `auraclimb-moai-variant-sideview.png`

**What's Different:**
- Moai shown from side instead of frontal
- More dynamic angle
- Arrow emerging from behind or above

**Pros:**
- Less static than frontal
- Unique silhouette
- More movement/direction
- Still instantly recognizable as moai

**Cons:**
- Slightly less iconic than frontal view
- May not read as clearly at 16px

**Best For:**
- If frontal feels too static
- Alternative social media icons
- Marketing variations

**Vibe:** Dynamic, directional, modern

---

### 2. Geometric Minimal
**File:** `auraclimb-moai-variant-geometric.png`

**What's Different:**
- Moai simplified to pure geometric shapes
- Ultra-minimal, almost abstract
- Very flat, contemporary design

**Pros:**
- Super clean scaling
- Modern startup aesthetic
- Abstract but recognizable
- Very 2026 design trends

**Cons:**
- Loses some cultural specificity
- Less immediately "moai-like"

**Best For:**
- Modern/startup positioning
- When you want minimal aesthetic
- Tech-forward branding

**Vibe:** Contemporary, clean, sophisticated

---

### 3. 3D Depth
**File:** `auraclimb-moai-variant-3d.png`

**What's Different:**
- Subtle 3D shading and highlights
- Arrow carved/integrated into moai
- More dimensional

**Pros:**
- Premium feel
- Adds depth without complexity
- Integrated arrow feels natural
- Modern but substantial

**Cons:**
- Slightly more complex (may not scale as cleanly to tiny sizes)
- 3D can date faster than flat

**Best For:**
- Premium positioning
- When flat feels too simple
- Desktop/larger contexts

**Vibe:** Premium, substantial, modern

---

### 4. Line Art
**File:** `auraclimb-moai-variant-lineart.png`

**What's Different:**
- Moai as outline/line art only
- Arrow solid inside the outline
- Artistic/illustrative approach

**Pros:**
- Unique aesthetic
- Arrow pops as solid shape
- Artistic/creative vibe
- Different from typical app icons

**Cons:**
- May be too artistic for mass appeal
- Lines need to be thick enough at small sizes

**Best For:**
- Creative positioning
- Alternative versions for merch
- Art/design-focused contexts

**Vibe:** Artistic, creative, different

---

### 5. Double Arrow (Symmetrical)
**File:** `auraclimb-moai-variant-double-arrow.png`

**What's Different:**
- Two upward arrows flanking moai
- Symmetrical composition
- More emphasis on upward movement

**Pros:**

- Bold and balanced
- Arrows hard to miss
- Powerful composition

**Cons:**

- Slightly less minimal

**Best For:**

- Competition branding

**Vibe:** Bold, powerful, competitive

**Pros:**

**Cons:**

- Might not read clearly at tiny sizes

- Standing out visually

### 7. Arrow as Base/Pedestal
**File:** `auraclimb-moai-variant-arrow-base.png`

**What's Different:**
- Large arrow beneath moai
- Arrow as foundation/pedestal
- Moai sits on top of arrow

**Pros:**
- Arrow more prominent
- Different composition
- Stable/grounded feeling
- Arrow = foundation

**Cons:**
- Less upward emphasis (arrow points up but is at bottom)
- Different from typical "arrow above" symbolism

**Best For:**
- When arrow deserves equal focus
- Stable/grounded positioning
- Alternative compositions

**Vibe:** Grounded, foundational, stable

### 10. Textured Stone

## Mix & Match Strategy

You don't have to use one variant for everything. Consider:

**Primary App Icon:** Variant 8 (Glow) - energetic, stands out
**Website/Marketing:** Variant 2 (Geometric) - clean, professional
**Social Media:** Original or Variant 8 - cultural + energetic
**Merchandise:** Variant 5 (Double Arrow) - bold on clothing
**Alternative/Special:** Variant 6 (Stacked) - unique moments

---

## Scale Testing

All variants designed to work at:
- 1024×1024px (App Store requirement)
- 512×512px (Play Store)
- 180×180px (iOS @3x)
- 120×120px (iOS @2x)
- 60×60px (iOS @1x)
- 32×32px (favicon)
- 16×16px (smallest usage)

**Best scalers:**
1. Variant 2 (Geometric) - simplest shapes
2. Variant 8 (Glow) - clear elements
3. Variant 5 (Double Arrow) - bold shapes
4. Original frontal - classic icon

**Requires more testing at tiny sizes:**
1. Variant 6 (Stacked) - complex
2. Variant 9 (Negative Space) - subtle
3. Variant 10 (Textured) - detail may be lost

## Decision Framework

**Choose Variant 8 (Glow) if:**
- You want maximum energy
- Competitive vibe is priority #1
- Need to stand out in app stores
- Target audience is young/energetic

**Choose Variant 2 (Geometric) if:**
- Clean/minimal is your aesthetic
- Want modern startup vibe
- Scaling to tiny sizes is critical
- Professional look is important

**Choose Variant 5 (Double Arrow) if:**
- Bold statement is your goal
- "Climbing" message must be obvious
- Not afraid to be in-your-face
- Competitive positioning is key

**Choose Original if:**
- Cultural authenticity is paramount
- Simple is better
- Already works perfectly
- Don't want to overthink it
