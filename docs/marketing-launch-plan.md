# Aura Launch Plan

**Days 1-3: Momentum**
- Daily challenge content

**Days 4-7: Amplification**

## Platform Strategy

### TikTok (Primary Growth Channel)

**Why:** Native audience understands aura language, viral potential, algorithm favors new accounts

**Content Mix:**
- 40% Challenge entries/examples
- 30% Leaderboard drama/updates
- 20% Tutorial/how-to
- 10% Community highlights

**Posting Frequency:** 3-5x daily during launch week, 2-3x daily sustained

**Ad Strategy:**
- Spark Ads on top-performing organic content
- Collection of creator-submitted posts
- Target: 18-30, interests in BeReal, TikTok trends, gaming

### Instagram

**Why:** Visual platform, Reels algorithm strong, established creator economy

**Content Mix:**
- Reels (same as TikTok)
- Stories (daily updates, polls, challenges)
- Feed posts (weekly top posts, winners)

**Posting Frequency:** 2-3 Reels daily, 5-10 Stories daily, 3-4 Feed posts weekly

**Ad Strategy:**
- Story ads with "Get the app" CTA
- Reels ads showing actual gameplay
- Carousel ads explaining the concept

### Twitter/X

**Why:** Text-based virality, meme culture, real-time updates

**Content Mix:**
- Leaderboard updates
- Battle results
- Community banter
- Challenge announcements
- Memes

**Posting Frequency:** 8-12x daily

**Ad Strategy:**
- Promoted tweets on viral posts
- Video ads showcasing competition

### YouTube (Secondary)

**Why:** Longer-form explainers, influencer partnerships

**Content Mix:**
- "What is Aura?" explainer
- Challenge compilation videos
- Weekly top posts compilation
- How to rank up guides

**Posting Frequency:** 2-3x weekly

## Paid Ads Strategy

### Budget Allocation (Example: $10k/month)

- TikTok: 40% ($4k)
- Instagram: 35% ($3.5k)
- Twitter: 15% ($1.5k)
- Google UAC: 10% ($1k)

### Campaign Types

**Discovery Campaigns (Week 1-2)**
- Objective: Installs + Awareness
- Creative: "What is Aura?" explainer style
- Budget: 50% of total

**Conversion Campaigns (Week 3+)**
- Objective: Installs + Engagement
- Creative: User-generated content, battle highlights
- Budget: 30% of total

**Retargeting (Week 2+)**
- Objective: Re-engagement
- Audience: Installed but inactive, visited landing page
- Budget: 20% of total

### Creative Testing

Test 3 angles:
1. Competition angle ("Think you can reach #1?")
2. Easy money angle ("Farm aura just by scrolling")
3. Social proof angle ("Everyone's climbing, where are you?")

Refresh creative every 3-4 days based on performance

## Influencer Strategy

### Micro-Influencers (10k-100k followers)

**Target:** 20-30 creators across platforms

**Approach:**
- Free early access
- Featured in-app if they hit milestones
- Rev share on referred installs (later phase)

**Content:**
- Authentic first-time experience
- Challenge participation
- Battle wins/losses

### Macro-Influencers (100k-1M followers)

**Target:** 3-5 carefully selected creators

**Approach:**
- Paid partnership ($500-2k depending on reach)
- Custom challenge creation
- Multi-post campaign

**Content:**
- Challenge announcement
- Participation video
- Results/winner announcement

### Selection Criteria:
- Audience overlap with Gen Z
- Previous success with app promos
- Natural fit with competitive/gaming content
- High engagement rate (>3%)

## Growth Loops

### Viral Loop #1: Shareable Wins

When user ranks up → prompt to share
- Auto-generated graphic with rank
- "I just hit #47 in Aura"
- One-tap share to Instagram Story

### Viral Loop #2: Battle Participation

When user's post enters a battle → notification
- "Your post is in a battle! Get your friends to vote"
- Share link to battle
- Voters get rewarded → they install app

### Viral Loop #3: Challenge FOMO

Daily challenges create recurring FOMO
- "Today's challenge: Most Unbothered"
- Users share their entries
- Non-users see posts → want to participate

## Risk Mitigation

### Content Moderation

**Risk:** Inappropriate content goes viral early
**Mitigation:**
- Pre-launch test moderation systems
- 24/7 on-call moderation team during launch week
- Auto-flag system with human review
- Clear community guidelines

### Technical Issues

**Risk:** App crashes under load
**Mitigation:**
- Load testing before launch
- Gradual rollout by region
- Backend monitoring and alerts
- Emergency response plan

### Low Adoption

**Risk:** Users don't understand concept
**Mitigation:**
- Strong onboarding flow in app
- Tutorial content everywhere
- Simplified messaging
- Incentivized early actions

### Competition from Users

**Risk:** Gaming the system, fake accounts
**Mitigation:**
- Rate limiting
- Account verification
- Aura event audit system
- Ban/suspend mechanisms ready day 1

## Emergency Playbook

### If growth stalls:

1. Double down on what's working
2. Launch emergency challenge with prizes
3. Activate influencer posts
4. Create controversial content
5. Host battle tournament

### If technical issues occur:

1. Pause paid ads immediately
2. Communicate transparently on social
3. Offer compensation (aura, titles)
4. Document and fix
5. Re-launch push

### If negative sentiment emerges:

1. Address directly and quickly
2. Make visible changes
3. Community call/AMA
4. Feature improvements
5. Rebuild trust through actions
